INTERACTION FIX

Updated interactive behavior for:
- About Me: dashboard topic cards open the corresponding full content panel.
- Achievements: dashboard cards open their content; achievement categories open their photo collections; each gallery photo opens the existing enlarged lightbox.
- Parents: family dashboard card opens all four family portraits and their details; each portrait opens a full-size photo viewer; each letter button opens its complete letter.
- All dashboard panels close with the close button, clicking the backdrop, or Escape.
- Photo viewer supports close, previous/next, and Escape.

The main page remains a fixed, non-scrolling dashboard. Only the opened content panel can scroll if its content is taller than the screen, so no information is cut off on smaller screens.
