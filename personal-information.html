A LITTLE ABOUT ME

My name is Ejelyn Cudal Garin, a BS Information Technology student majoring in Networking and Cybersecurity. I am a curious, determined, and observant person who enjoys learning about technology, philosophy, human behavior, and the world around me.

I have always been someone who asks questions, reflects deeply, and tries to understand things beyond what is immediately visible. I enjoy writing, journaling, reading, exploring new ideas, and expressing my thoughts through stories. I believe that there is always something to learn from every experience, even the ones that challenge us the most.

My journey has not been a straight path. I have experienced uncertainty, academic struggles, changing dreams, and moments when I questioned myself and my direction. But through these experiences, I have slowly learned to understand who I am and what I genuinely want to pursue.

Above all, my faith in God remains one of my strongest foundations. I am still a work in progress, and I do not claim to have all the answers. I am simply a woman who wants to keep learning, growing, serving, and becoming someone my younger self would be proud of.

“I may be quiet in how I express myself, but I am driven by the depth of what I believe, the things I dream of, and the person I continue to become.”

“Use Knowledge to Serve.” — I want to use what I learn, what I experience, and what I become to make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

HOBBIES & CREATIVE INTERESTS

The things that make me, me

Outside my academic responsibilities, I enjoy exploring the things that allow me to understand myself and the world around me.

Writing and journaling are among the ways I process my thoughts and emotions. Sometimes, there are things I find difficult to explain in conversations, so I turn to writing to give those thoughts a place to exist. Through words, I can reflect on my experiences, question my beliefs, and understand different parts of myself.

I also enjoy reading, exploring philosophical questions, learning about human behavior, and discovering new ideas. I am curious about how people think, how society works, and how our experiences influence the way we see life.

Technology is another growing interest of mine. As an IT student, I want to explore networking, cybersecurity, coding, website development, and digital content creation. I enjoy the idea of building something from an idea and learning how technology can be used creatively and meaningfully.

I am also interested in storytelling, video editing, nature, and social advocacy, particularly conversations surrounding education, women's rights, equality, and human dignity.

Through these interests, I hope to keep discovering new ways to express myself, develop my abilities, and connect with people.

For me, hobbies are not simply ways to spend free time. They are also opportunities to explore who I am and what I might become.
