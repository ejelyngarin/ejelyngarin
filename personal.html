My story

About me.

My name is Ejelyn Cudal Garin, a BS Information Technology student majoring in Networking and Cybersecurity. I am a curious, determined, and observant person who enjoys learning about technology, philosophy, human behavior, and the world around me.

I have always been someone who asks questions, reflects deeply, and tries to understand things beyond what is immediately visible. I enjoy writing, journaling, reading, exploring new ideas, and expressing my thoughts through stories.

My journey has not been a straight path. I have experienced uncertainty, academic struggles, changing dreams, and moments when I questioned myself and my direction. But through these experiences, I have slowly learned to understand who I am and what I genuinely want to pursue.

Above all, my faith in God remains one of my strongest foundations. I am still a work in progress. I am simply a woman who wants to keep learning, growing, serving, and becoming someone my younger self would be proud of.
